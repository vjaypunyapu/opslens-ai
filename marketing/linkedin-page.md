## About Section (2,000 char max)

---

Engineering teams are drowning in alert noise. P1 incidents take over an hour to diagnose. The wrong team gets paged. Engineers piece together root causes manually from logs, Jira, and GitHub — while users are already complaining.

OpsLens AI fixes this.

We turn raw logs into instant, AI-diagnosed incident briefs — automatically routed to the right team via Slack, with full context from your existing tools.

**Here's how it works:**
- Your logs hit OpsLens in real time
- Our AI detects errors, deduplicates noise, and builds an incident signature
- We pull context from Jira tickets, GitHub PRs, and Slack threads automatically
- An AI root cause analysis is generated before your on-call engineer even opens Slack
- Smart routing rules send the alert to the right channel — payment team, infra team, or on-call
- A structured RRT (Rapid Response Triage) brief is ready the moment your team engages

The result: incidents that used to take 90 minutes to diagnose resolve in under 15.

OpsLens AI is built for startups and growth-stage engineering teams (5–50 engineers) who ship fast and can't afford the overhead of enterprise ITSM tooling. We integrate with Slack, GitHub, and Jira — no rip-and-replace required.

Currently in private beta. Join the waitlist at opslens.ai.
