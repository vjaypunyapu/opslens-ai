### FAQ

**Do I need a credit card to start?**
No. The 14-day free trial requires no credit card. You'll only be asked for payment when you're ready to continue.

**What counts as a "log event"?**
A log event is a single log line ingested by OpsLens. Most services generate 100K–500K log events per day.

**What happens if I exceed my monthly log limit?**
We'll send you a heads-up at 80% and 100% usage. Overage is billed at the per-event rate shown in your plan. You can also upgrade at any time.

**Can I change plans mid-month?**
Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**Is there an annual discount?**
Yes — annual billing saves 20%. Contact us at hello@opslens.ai.

**What integrations are included?**
All plans include Slack, GitHub, and Jira at no additional cost. AWS CloudWatch and Datadog webhook ingestion are also included. Additional native integrations are available on Enterprise.

**What does "AI diagnosis" include?**
Each AI diagnosis call generates a root cause hypothesis, severity assessment, contributing factors, and remediation suggestions. Starter includes 50 per month; Growth includes unlimited.

## 7. Brand Voice Guide

### Tone

- **Direct.** No fluff. Engineers respect brevity.
- **Technical.** Name the thing: "RAG enrichment", "routing rules", "P1 incident". Don't dumb it down.
- **Confident without hype.** We don't say "revolutionary" or "game-changing". We say "resolves incidents in 15 minutes".
- **Empathetic.** We've been the on-call engineer. We write like we have.

### Do Say

- "turns noisy logs into actionable incident briefs"
- "routes to the right team"
- "AI root cause analysis"
- "resolves incidents faster"
- "context-first alerting"

### Don't Say

- "revolutionize" / "game-changer" / "disrupt"
- "AI-powered" without specifics (say *what* the AI does)
- "easy to use" (say *what* is easy and *how fast*)
- "best in class" (unprovable; use a specific metric instead)

### Audience Vocabulary

| Audience | Language to use |
|---------|----------------|
| Engineers | MTTR, P1, RRT brief, alert fatigue, on-call, routing rules, log ingestion |
| CTOs/VPs | Downtime cost, team efficiency, engineering velocity, SOC 2 |
| Investors | TAM, wedge, usage-based model, AI-native, LLM cost curve |
