Team alert routing

Route alerts to the right team's Slack channel based on what's in the error. Rules are evaluated in priority order. Add one team per service area.

💡 Add a catch-all team (e.g. "Eng On-Call") with service pattern .* at priority 999 to ensure no alert is ever missed.
